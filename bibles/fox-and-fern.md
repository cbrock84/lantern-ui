## Brand vision

**Established.** "Kid-friendly workbooks and activity books that treat children
like capable, curious learners, and turn practice into something they actually
want to pick up." Fox & Fern is Lantern Learn's kid-facing imprint for everyday
workbook practice. Holly & Hare is the rigor-leaning one.

**To define.** The one belief every Fox & Fern book should leave a child with.
Rocket & Raven's is "I can figure this out."

## Audience

**Established.** Pre-K through Grade 5, ages 4 to 10, parents buying for
summer and at-home practice.

## Voice and tone

**Established** (from site copy):
- Warm and playful.
- Treats kids like capable learners, and treats summers like summer.
- Practice should fit around real life ("before the pool, not instead of it").
- Instructions face the child. Parents need no teaching background.

**To define.**
- Sample phrases to use and to avoid.
- How the books address the child (second person? a narrator?).
- Humor: how much, and what kind.

## Visual style

**Established.**
- Palette: fox orange `#D77939` (deep auburn `#752E17`), fern green `#5C8C44` /
  `#527143`, warm cream backgrounds.
- Fonts: Fredoka (display), Nunito (body).
- The logo shows a fox.
- "Bright, uncluttered pages designed for real kids on real summers."

**To define.**

- The art prompt text, in the same form as

## Series

### Tomorrow Trail Activity Workbooks

**Established.**
- Six grade-band books: Pre-K→K, K→1, 1→2, 2→3, 3→4 and 4→5.
- 12 weeks of daily practice, 15 to 20 minutes a day.
- Covers math, reading, writing and hands-on activities.
- Difficulty is progressive, to bridge the summer slide.
- Answer keys are in the back. Free 4-page sample PDFs are available on most
  books.
- Moving online as free digital workbooks with an optional free PDF (D10).

**To define.**
- A day template (what a typical day contains, in order).
- Recurring page types.
- Whether characters appear in the workbook pages.
