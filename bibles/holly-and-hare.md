# Holly & Hare bible

**Status:** Skeleton v0.1 (2026-09-23).
- **Established:** taken from the published site (hollyandhare.com).
- **To define:** needs the owner.

Holly & Hare has **no character cast** (D14): its bible is a voice, design and
puzzle-writing guide. Shared rules:
[`../standards/content-standards.md`](../standards/content-standards.md).

## Brand vision

**Established.** "Rigorous workbooks for kids ready to think hard. Gifted prep,
critical thinking, and test prep, with quiet design, tough problems, satisfying
'I figured it out' moments." The imprint serves "the family that wants more than
the everyday workbook". It is Lantern Learn's rigor-leaning imprint: "pages are
quieter, problems are harder, and the design is more grown-up."

## Audience

**Established.** Grades 3 to 5, ages 8 to 11. The books aim at
gifted-program preparation, critical thinking and academic rigor.

## Voice and tone

**Established:**
- Respectful and calm.
- Names its methods honestly.
- Never promises outcomes: "no workbook can promise a score outcome".
- Explains how a strong thinker approaches a problem.

**To define.**
- Sample phrasing for strategy pages, hints and answer explanations.
- How encouragement is phrased without being childish.

## Design

**Established:**
- "No cartoon mascots, no gimmicks."
- Spacious one-puzzle-per-page layouts and clear instructions.
- Palette: navy `#1A2C4F`, cream `#F1EEE2`, holly green `#214A35`, gold
  `#C9A859`, and berry `#BB3939` for sparing emphasis.
- Fonts: Cardo (display), Crimson Text (body), Inter (UI).

**To define.** Whether illustrations are used at all (diagrams only?), and their
style.

## Puzzle-writing guide

**Established structure** (Gifted & Talented Puzzle Prep):
- Each section opens with a strategy explainer: "how a strong thinker sees
  this".
- A worked example models the thought process, not only the answer.
- Difficulty ramps deliberately: warm-up, then build, then challenge.
- Each section ends with a timed Challenge Set.
- The full answer key at the back includes reasoning explanations.

**Established question types:**
- figure matrices;
- paper folding and mental rotation;
- verbal analogies and classification with grade-level vocabulary;
- quantitative reasoning (number series, equation puzzles, logic grids).

**To define.**
- An item-writing spec per question type: what makes an item valid, how many
  distractors, and what makes distractors plausible.
- Difficulty calibration per grade.
- How online answer checking should explain a wrong choice.

## Honesty and test-prep disclaimers

**Established.** Built on the public reasoning frameworks behind CogAT® and
NNAT®. It is "not affiliated with, licensed from, or endorsed by the publishers
of those tests", and says so wherever it matters. This is a hard rule for every
Holly & Hare product.

## Series

- **Established.** Gifted & Talented Puzzle Prep, Grades 3, 4 and 5. Moving
  online as free digital workbooks with an optional free PDF (D10).
- **Named on the site but not defined:** Critical Thinking Lab, Common Core Test
  Prep, Advanced Math Adventures. **To define:** scope, grades and format for
  each.
- **Short stories (D10). To define:** whether Holly & Hare publishes stories at
  all, given its puzzle focus. One option is logic-puzzle stories with no cast.
