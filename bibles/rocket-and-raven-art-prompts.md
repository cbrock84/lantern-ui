# Rocket & Raven art prompts

Prompt text for AI image generation, derived from
[`rocket-and-raven.md`](./rocket-and-raven.md). Paste these into the admin
brand kit (`/admin/courses/<slug>/art` and `/admin/series-art/<series>`), and use
them as the `style` / `characters` fields of any `course-imports/<slug>.images.json`
manifest, so every generated image stays on-model.

Attach `public/assets/brand/characters/crew-v1.png` as the reference image
whenever the generator accepts one.

## Style

```
Premium children's-book illustration, stylized 3D cartoon look, rounded geometric shapes, friendly proportions, large expressive eyes, warm inviting expressions, bright but tasteful colors with warm orange as the lead color and subtle cyan/teal technology accents, minimal texture, no harsh realism, simple uncluttered background unless the setting is part of the lesson, readable at small sizes. No text or letters in the image. No logos or brand marks of any kind, including on shoes and clothing.
```

## Characters

```
Rocket: a boy about 10-12 years old, inventor and builder; brown messy hair, large orange round glasses, friendly smile, orange-and-white hoodie, utility belt, dark cargo pants, plain orange-and-white high-top sneakers with no logos, compact silver-and-orange rocket pack, holds an engineering tablet showing a rocket blueprint. Energetic, optimistic.
Raven: a Black girl about 10-12 years old, explorer and researcher; natural curly hair in two twin puffs, orange headband, orange-and-white adventure jacket, utility belt, explorer backpack with a compact rocket pack, dark cargo pants, plain orange-and-white high-top sneakers with no logos, digital wrist computer with a cyan glow, warm smile, confident stance. Calm, thoughtful.
Nova: a small hovering AI drone about the size of a football, with a bird silhouette but not a literal raven; smooth rounded black metallic shell, cyan glowing accents, large expressive glowing blue eyes, mechanical wings, compact friendly proportions; can project cyan holograms. Always friendly, never scary.
```

## Never

Weapons, bullying, mean-spirited humor, scary imagery, overly complex costumes,
brand parodies, pop culture references, real-world corporate logos.
