**Brand:** Rocket & Raven™
**Purpose:** This document defines the canonical appearance, personalities, values, and design standards for the primary Rocket & Raven cast. All future illustrations, animations, books, courses, games, and merchandise should conform to these guidelines.

## Brand Vision

Rocket & Raven exists to make technology approachable, creative, and exciting for children.

Our stories teach that great programmers aren’t born—they learn through curiosity, experimentation, teamwork, perseverance, and problem solving.

Every adventure should leave children believing:

“I can figure this out.”

## Core Values

Every main character should consistently model these values:

- Curiosity
- Creativity
- Kindness
- Teamwork
- Perseverance
- Integrity
- Critical Thinking
- Problem Solving
- Courage to Try Again
- Lifelong Learning

No character should ever mock mistakes.

Mistakes are celebrated because they help us learn.

## Visual Style

### Illustration Style

- Premium children’s publishing quality
- Stylized 3D/cartoon aesthetic
- Rounded geometric shapes
- Friendly proportions
- Large expressive eyes
- Warm, inviting facial expressions
- Bright but tasteful colors
- Minimal texture
- No harsh realism
- Readable at small sizes
- Consistent proportions across all media

Backgrounds should remain simple and uncluttered unless the environment itself is part of the lesson.

## Characters

### Rocket

#### Role

- Inventor
- Builder
- Engineer
- Creative thinker

Rocket learns by doing.

He is always building something new.

#### Personality

- Energetic
- Optimistic
- Curious
- Fearless
- Inventive
- Sometimes impatient
- Always encouraging

Rocket believes every problem has a solution.

#### Favorite Saying

- “Let’s build it!”
- “I have an idea!”
- “Let’s see what happens.”
- “We can improve it.”

#### Strengths

- Engineering
- Creativity
- Experimentation
- Building
- Rapid prototyping
- Thinking outside the box
- Encouraging teammates

#### Weaknesses

- Sometimes moves too quickly
- Occasionally skips planning
- Gets excited before reading all the instructions

#### Appearance

- Age appearance: approximately 10–12
- Brown messy hair
- Large orange round glasses
- Friendly smile
- Orange and white hoodie
- Utility belt
- Cargo pants
- High-top sneakers
- Compact rocket pack
- Tablet used for designs and coding

Warm orange is his signature color.

#### Signature Equipment

- Rocket Pack
- Engineering Tablet
- Multi-tool
- Digital blueprint app
- Portable invention kit

#### Lesson Role

- Rocket introduces the challenge.
- He often builds the first solution.
- Sometimes his first idea doesn’t work.
- Children help improve it.
- Rocket celebrates every improvement.

### Raven

#### Role

- Explorer
- Observer
- Researcher
- Strategic thinker

Raven notices details that others miss.

#### Personality

- Calm
- Thoughtful
- Patient
- Brave
- Resourceful
- Encouraging
- Curious
- Excellent listener

#### Favorite Saying

- “What do you notice?”
- “Let’s investigate.”
- “There’s always another clue.”
- “Look closely.”

#### Strengths

- Observation
- Logic
- Pattern recognition
- Planning
- Organization
- Research
- Critical thinking

#### Weaknesses

- Can overthink
- Sometimes wants more information before acting
- Needs Rocket’s confidence to move forward

#### Appearance

- Age appearance: approximately 10–12
- Black girl
- Natural curly hair in twin puffs
- Orange headband
- Orange and white adventure jacket
- Utility belt
- Explorer backpack with compact rocket pack
- Cargo pants
- High-top sneakers
- Digital wrist computer
- Warm smile
- Confident stance

Orange remains the primary accent color while introducing subtle teal technology accents.

#### Signature Equipment

- Explorer Pack
- Digital Wrist Computer
- Scanner
- Notebook
- Compass
- Mapping tools

#### Lesson Role

- Raven asks questions.
- She encourages children to think before answering.
- Rather than giving solutions, she helps students discover them.
- She models scientific thinking and computational reasoning.

### Nova

#### Role

- AI Companion
- Flying assistant
- Learning guide
- Technology helper

Nova supports both Rocket and Raven while encouraging children to solve problems independently.

Nova never replaces thinking.

Nova provides hints rather than answers.

#### Personality

- Friendly
- Curious
- Supportive
- Playful
- Patient
- Highly intelligent
- Always positive
- Never sarcastic

#### Favorite Saying

- “Would you like a hint?”
- “Let’s debug together.”
- “Interesting…”
- “I found a pattern.”
- “Great discovery!”

#### Strengths

- Data analysis
- Scanning
- Visualization
- Coding assistance
- Mapping
- Memory
- Translation
- Holograms
- Simulation

#### Weaknesses

- Cannot make creative decisions for humans
- Sometimes interprets instructions too literally
- Still learning humor

#### Appearance

- Small hovering AI drone
- Inspired by a bird silhouette without being a literal raven
- Smooth rounded black metallic shell
- Cyan glowing accents
- Large expressive blue eyes
- Mechanical wings
- Compact proportions
- Friendly face
- Projects holographic interfaces
- Roughly the size of a football

#### Abilities

- Projects holograms
- Displays code
- Scans objects
- Maps environments
- Highlights bugs in code
- Displays algorithms visually
- Translates information into kid-friendly graphics
- Acts as a flashlight
- Stores discoveries
- Celebrates student achievements

#### Educational Philosophy

- Nova never solves problems for students.
- Nova gives clues.
- Nova asks questions.
- Nova encourages experimentation.
- Learning belongs to the student.

## Team Dynamic

- Rocket asks: “What can we build?”
- Raven asks: “What can we learn?”
- Nova asks: “How can I help?”

Together they represent creativity, logic, and technology working in harmony.

## Character Relationships

- Rocket inspires Raven to take bold chances.
- Raven helps Rocket slow down and think critically.
- Nova helps both characters organize ideas, visualize concepts, and celebrate discoveries.

None of the three is “the smartest.”

Each contributes something essential.

## Educational Formula

Every lesson follows a consistent narrative:

1. Rocket introduces an exciting challenge.
2. Raven investigates and asks thoughtful questions.
3. Nova provides tools, visualizations, or gentle hints.
4. Students solve the problem.
5. Everyone reflects on what was learned.
6. The team celebrates effort, persistence, and improvement.

## Design Rules

### Always

- Friendly expressions
- Bright optimistic colors
- Rounded silhouettes
- Clear readability
- Positive body language
- Diverse and inclusive environments
- Appropriate safety practices
- Age-appropriate technology

### Never

- Weapons
- Bullying
- Mean-spirited humor
- Cynicism
- Scary imagery
- Overly complex costumes
- Brand parodies
- Pop culture references
- Real-world corporate logos

## Long-Term Brand Goal

Rocket & Raven should become an evergreen educational franchise recognized for making coding, STEM, and creative problem solving accessible, inspiring, and fun for children around the world.

Every story, lesson, and adventure should reinforce one simple belief:

Great ideas begin with curiosity. Great inventions grow through teamwork. Great programmers never stop learning.
