# Lantern Learn roadmap

Set by the owner on 2026-10-10 (D145). The long-term scope is K-12 through
adult training; young children are the early focus, not the ceiling. Every
course stays free with no ads or tracking (D106, D138).

Rank new work against the signals below. Re-scan them about quarterly and
update this file in the same change set as the decision that changes it.

## Reach and revenue (courses stay free)

- Register as an education savings account (ESA) vendor in two or three
  states (ClassWallet: Arizona, Alabama, Idaho; Odyssey: Texas, Utah,
  Louisiana, Iowa, Georgia, Wyoming, Missouri; Tennessee from October 2026)
  for paid add-ons such as printed packs, live help or a family pass.
  Approved for research on 2026-10-10 (D146); the state-by-state plan is being
  written and will be added here.
- Pitch microschools directly: about 75,000 schools and 1.5 million students,
  87% serving ages 5-11 (National Microschooling Center, 2026). The platform
  already has classrooms and assignments.
- Say on the sites that read-aloud, word highlighting and short daily blocks
  help learners with dyslexia, working memory and math anxiety (top teacher
  searches, Google Trends 2025).

## Content direction by imprint

- **Fox & Fern (reading, life skills):** explicit phonics and decodable text.
  Science-of-reading laws exist in 42 states and DC. Home Helpers and the
  Kitchen course carry the life-skills lane.
- **Rocket & Raven (STEM, coding):** Code Crew grows upward through grade 12;
  AI Explorers joins it. Science Launch, Body Lab, Backyard Explorer and
  Everyday Machines carry science.
