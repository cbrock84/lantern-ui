# Source catalog

Outside educational sites we have checked, what their licenses let us do, and
where they fit. Course facts still go in each course's `SOURCES.md`; this file
is the shared shortlist a course author checks first. See D306.

How to use it:

- **Reuse** means the license lets us copy or adapt the material into a lesson.
  Keep the attribution the license asks for, next to the material.
- **Fact source** means we can check facts against it and list it in a
  course's `SOURCES.md`. We write our own text.
- **Link only** means everything is reserved. We may point families to it
  (for example from a resources page), never copy text, art or activities.
- **Avoid** means don't link or cite it (closed, wrong site, or ads and
  tracking that conflict with our no-ads stance).
- "NC" (NonCommercial) material may be adapted: our courses are free and
  ad-free, so we treat them as noncommercial (D307). "ND" (NoDerivatives) material stays link
  only.
- "SA" (ShareAlike) material makes the adapted part carry the same license, so
  it goes in a clearly marked block with its own license line.
- Licenses change. PhET changed its license on 2026-03-29. Re-open the license
  URL before relying on an entry older than six months.

Checked 2026-10-12 unless noted. "Partly" means the site's own page blocked
automated access and the license was confirmed another way (named in Notes).
